\section{Dataset and Split Construction}

\paragraph{Corpora.} Evaluation uses Rainforest Connection rainforest soundscapes
(CC BY-NC 4.0), which supply chainsaw and background clips annotated with a
recording site. The closed-set training lineage additionally draws on public
forest and urban corpora: FSC22 \citep{bandara2023fsc22}, ESC-50/FSD50K-derived
negatives, DCASE machine sounds, and Kaggle chainsaw, gunshot, vehicle, and
wildfire sets. No model evaluated for a cross-site claim was trained on any RFCx
clip; we verified this directly over the training manifests, which contain zero
rows with an RFCx source for every public-trained model in
Table~\ref{tab:supp-ladder}.

\paragraph{Site support.} Fourteen RFCx sites appear in the corpus, but most
contribute background only. Four carry enough of both classes for a per-site
estimate and are used throughout: warsi ($412$ chainsaw / $198$ background test
clips), tambopata ($41/72$), romania ($30/15$), pooks ($10/12$). The remaining
sites contribute background to training and to the representation probe, which
uses seven sites with at least $20$ clips each.

\paragraph{Leave-one-site-out construction.} For a held-out site $S$, every clip
recorded at $S$ becomes the test split; train and validation are drawn from the
remaining sites plus public data. Validation is used only to calibrate the
decision threshold, never to select a model.

\paragraph{Leakage audit.} RFCx clips are fixed-length windows cut from longer
recordings, so filename-level disjointness is insufficient: two windows of one
recording could otherwise straddle a split. We therefore key on the source
recording, stripping the trailing \texttt{\_<start>-<end>} window index, and
verify three properties per fold: zero held-out-site clips in train, zero in
validation, and zero shared source recordings between any two splits. All four
folds pass on all three counts.

\section{Implementation Details}

\paragraph{Detector hyperparameters.} Covariance shrinkage $\alpha = 0.1$ toward
the diagonal with $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$ ridge; background-CDF quantile grid of
$1001$ points; similarity floor $\theta = 0.5$; softmax temperature $0.1$;
false-positive budget $\beta = 0.10$. These are defaults carried unchanged from
the deployed platform rather than tuned per fold, which is why the paper reports
a single operating point rather than a sweep.

\paragraph{Embeddings.} Site-trained CNN: $64$-d pre-classifier feature, log-mel
input, $16$\,kHz, $4$\,s clips, $64$ mels. AudioSet CNN14: $2048$-d, $32$\,kHz,
via \texttt{panns\_inference} \citep{kong2020panns}. BirdNET V2.4: $1024$-d, via
\texttt{birdnetlib} \citep{kahl2021birdnet} on the LiteRT runtime. All are frozen;
no gradient reaches any encoder.

\paragraph{Supervised probe.} Logistic regression, \texttt{max\_iter}~$=2000$,
balanced class weights, on standardized features fit on the training split only.
The decision threshold is the $1-\beta$ quantile of validation-background scores.

\paragraph{Randomness and compute.} Bootstrap resampling ($1000$ draws) and
prototype subsampling use fixed seeds. Experiments run on CPU/Apple-Silicon under
macOS with PyTorch~2.7; a full regeneration of every number in the paper takes
approximately two hours, dominated by embedding extraction, which is cached.

\paragraph{Where BirdNET's label-free detector fails.} Under the unsupervised
detector, BirdNET flags nothing at any site ($0.000$ recall) despite
threshold-free AUC as high as $0.821$ on romania. The cause is visible in the
score distribution: BirdNET's background embedding cloud is tight enough that the
$1-\beta$ quantile of the training-site background Mahalanobis distance lands
above nearly all held-out-site scores, so the transferred threshold admits
nothing. This is the same operating-point transfer failure diagnosed for the
closed-set classifier, arising here without any supervision, and it is why the
paper reports recall at a fixed budget alongside AUC rather than AUC alone.

\paragraph{Honest-unknown holdout.} Removing chainsaw's prototype and re-scoring
the held-out site's chainsaw clips leaves flagging unchanged by construction, and
$100\%$ of flagged clips are predicted \emph{unknown} with mean unknown mass
$1.0$ at every site with held-out chainsaws. With prototypes for other classes
present, none of that mass is misattributed to them.

\section{Reproduction}

The code and data supplement contains the detector, both evaluation harnesses,
the plotting scripts, all report files backing every number in the paper, the
leave-one-site-out manifests, and the two CNN artifacts. Audio is not
redistributed: the evaluation corpus is third-party under a NonCommercial
license, so we ship manifests naming every clip instead. Every table and figure
regenerates from the committed reports without audio; full re-runs additionally
require the public corpus. The mathematics is NumPy-only and covered by $123$
unit tests that run without the audio stack.
